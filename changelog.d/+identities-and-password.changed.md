An identity is an identifier of a kind bound to a profile, `(kind, value)`,
not the subject of a method: a method names its kind, `IDENTIFIER_KIND`,
`nickname`, `email`, so the methods of one kind share the identifiers. The
password is of the profile, one whatever identifier it signs in by: its hash
is the secret of the uid, `secrets.put(uid, secret)`, and `AuthPassword`, the
slot `password` of the methods, hashes and verifies it, its group of the
policies `policies_set` checking a new one by whatever method.
