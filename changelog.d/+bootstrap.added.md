Started ingot-auth, an addon of Ingots for the sign-up, the sign-in and the sessions of the users of an application, for `ingots` 0.32.1 and later 0.32 releases. Its skeleton: `AuthProject`, the project of the defaults with the addon's `AuthSettings`, whose `AuthConfigs` add `AUTH_LOGGING_LEVEL`, `AuthLogging`, which adds the logger `ingot_auth` at that level, and the `auth` unit, `AuthUnit`, empty for now; `python -m ingot_auth` runs it on its own. The development automation of the ecosystem comes with it: `make check` (lint, tests on the working tree and on the built wheel for every supported Python, coverage, the Sphinx documentation, changelog check), towncrier news fragments, and the `ci` and `release` workflows.
