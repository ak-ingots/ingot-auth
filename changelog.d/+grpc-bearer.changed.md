A call over gRPC runs for the caller of the session of its token, the
header `authorization: Bearer <token>`, with the permissions its groups
grant, and for the anonymous one with no token: `_get_caller()` of the
servicers is asynchronous, and the administration lets in the users its
groups grant, no more the anonymous caller alone until a subclass told
otherwise. A token of no open session is `UNAUTHENTICATED`,
`SESSION_NOT_FOUND`, of one expired `SESSION_EXPIRED` with the reason. The
metadata and the methods run for the anonymous caller, whatever the call
bears.
