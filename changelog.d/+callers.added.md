The group `grp_anonymous` of the core, `AuthAnonymousGroup`, of every
caller that tells no one, none stored, as `grp_signed_in` is of every
signed-in user: it grants what anyone may of the auth, as its units tell
it, the flags of the sign-up and of the sign-in, those of the methods
their toggles open, and the reading of the metadata,
`auth.metadata.get_anonymous_permissions()`; an application grants anyone
more, or less, by its own class in the slot. `AuthProject` takes
`AuthOperationsUnit`, whose `AuthCallersProvider` gives the anonymous
caller of the project, `operations.callers.get_anonymous()`, what the
group grants. A signed-in user is not a member: it has what its groups
grant alone. A membership of `grp_anonymous` is refused as one of
`grp_signed_in`, the message of both `the group '<name>' keeps no members`.
