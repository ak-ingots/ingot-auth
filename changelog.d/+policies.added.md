The policies of what a user types, each an operation taking the part of
its parameters its protocol tells and raising `AuthPolicyViolationError`
with its name when the value breaks it: the length and the characters, by
the sets of Unicode, in the core, configured by the `CONFIG` of their
class, which a subclass replaces; an application writes its own as any
operation. The policies of a field of a flow are a group of a method, its
slot `policies_<flow>_<field>`, a `Factories` of ingots of the type the
method gives, `AuthNicknameSignUpPolicies(AppNicknameLength)`, empty until
an application gives policies to it in a subclass, so mypy refuses a
policy taking what the group does not give. The method runs the group by
`_check()`, every policy to its end, and a value breaking some raises
`AuthMethodInvalidError`, naming the field and every policy broken, in the
order of the group.
