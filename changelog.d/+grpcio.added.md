The auth over gRPC under the extra `grpcio`: a service for the tags, one for the attributes and a public one for the metadata, each a binding of its own, with the permissions of each in an enum of its contract (ADR 0009 of the ecosystem). The permissions of the addon are split by the sets of operations: `AuthTagsPermissions`, `AuthAttrsPermissions` and `AuthMetadataPermissions`, in the domains `auth.profile.tags`, `auth.profile.attrs` and `auth.metadata`, instead of `AuthPermissions`. `AuthItemInvalidError` tells an unacceptable id or name from a taken one.
